The graph applies (pixel - 127.5) / 128 internally.
Output: Identity, 128-dimensional embedding; normalized by the app before cosine comparison.

The app implements Vision landmark alignment and a basic randomized head-motion
challenge. These are separate from the model and are not a certified presentation
attack detection or Face ID implementation. Thresholds require device validation.
